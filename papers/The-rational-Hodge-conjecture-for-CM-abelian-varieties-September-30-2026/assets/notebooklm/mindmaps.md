## How the proof works

**Hodge Conjecture & Related Tensors**

- CM Abelian Varieties
  - Rational Hodge Conjecture Proof
    - Betti cycle-class map clB is surjective
    - Holds for products and powers of CM abelian varieties
  - Core Proof Strategy
    - Reduction to four-factor relations (v1 + v2 = v3 + v4)
    - Surface period criterion (degree-one Hodge maps)
    - Theta differentials on compact arithmetic quotients of complex two-ball
    - Weak approximation of Hermitian plane decompositions
    - Arithmetic Hecke endomorphisms and Frobenius relations
  - Consequences
    - Generalized Hodge Conjecture for CM abelian varieties
    - Tate Conjecture for abelian varieties over finite fields
    - Hodge Standard Conjecture in arbitrary characteristic
    - Specialization of Hodge classes on good reduction varieties
- Products of K3 Surfaces
  - Main Result
    - Rational Hodge conjecture holds for finite products of projective K3 surfaces
    - Applies to distinct or repeated factors with any Picard number/periods
  - Key Theoretical Tools
    - Kuga-Satake correspondences (weight-two to weight-one translation)
    - Homological Mirror Symmetry (Fukaya categories and mirror quartics/stori)
    - Graded Lagrangian immersions and disk-moduli Stokes identity
    - Hochschild cap-action and semiregularity deformation (Buchweitz-Flenner/Pridham)
    - Degree-four spin tensors and grid transport
- Weil Classes & Abelian Powers
  - Split Weil Sixfolds
    - Imaginary quadratic action with hyperbolic signature (3,3)
    - Rational Hodge conjecture holds in every codimension on all self-powers
  - Abelian Varieties ≤ 5 Dimensions
    - Admitting an imaginary-quadratic action
    - Rational Hodge conjecture holds on all self-powers
  - Methodological Steps
    - Determinant criterion for derived Hodge groups
    - Supply of quadratic determinants via auxiliary CM elliptic factors
    - Secant-sheaf constructions and Fourier-Mukai transforms
    - Algebraicity of the hyperbolic Weil plane
