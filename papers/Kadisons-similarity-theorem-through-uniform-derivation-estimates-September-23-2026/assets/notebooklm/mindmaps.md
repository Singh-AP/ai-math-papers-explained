## How the proof works

**Kadison's Similarity Theorem**

- Main Results
  - Similarity Theorem (Theorem 1.1)
    - Resolves Kadison's Conjecture positively
    - Bounded algebra homomorphism π: A → B(H) is similar to a *-homomorphism
    - Exists bounded invertible S such that ρ(a) = Sπ(a)S⁻¹ is a *-homomorphism
    - No separability, nuclearity, or normality conditions required
  - Uniform Commutator Estimate (Theorem 1.2)
    - Absolute constant C controls matrix amplifications
    - Bound: ||[Y(h), X]|| ≤ C * gP(Y) * ||X||
    - Uniform in matrix size h, algebra P, and Hilbert space K
  - Universal Hyperreflexivity (Corollary 1.3)
    - Every unital von Neumann algebra is hyperreflexive
    - Universal constant at most 2C
    - dist(T, M) ≤ 2C * αM(T)
