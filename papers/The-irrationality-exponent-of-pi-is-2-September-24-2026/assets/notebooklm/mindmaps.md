## How the proof works

**The Irrationality Exponent of Pi is 2**

- Main Result (Theorem 1.1)
  - Statement
    - µ(π) = 2
    - |π - p/q| ≥ q^-ν for q ≥ Q(ν) and ν > 2
  - Key Characteristics
    - Applies to unreduced fractions
    - Ineffective threshold Q(ν)
    - Differs from bounded continued-fraction quotients
- Consequences & Corollaries
  - Flint–Hills Series (Corollary 1.2)
    - Proves convergence of ∑ 1/(n^3 sin^2 n)
    - Satisfies Meiburg's condition µ(π) < 5/2
  - Generalized Flint–Hills Series (Corollary 5.2)
    - Series ∑ 1/(n^a |sin n|^b)
    - Converges if and only if a > max{1, b}
- Proof Methodology
