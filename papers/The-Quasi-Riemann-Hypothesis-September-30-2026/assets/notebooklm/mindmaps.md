## The Riemann Hypothesis landscape (papers + Wikipedia)

**Riemann Hypothesis**

- Core Concept & Definition
  - Conjecture on Riemann Zeta Function Zeros
  - Proposed by Bernhard Riemann (1859)
  - Trivial Zeros: Negative even integers (-2, -4, -6, ...)
  - Non-trivial Zeros: Lie on the Critical Line Re(s) = 1/2
  - Critical Strip: 0 < Re(s) < 1
  - Status: Hilbert's 8th Problem & Millennium Prize Problem ($1M)
- Riemann Zeta Function ζ(s)
  - Sum Definition: ∑ (1/n^s) for Re(s) > 1
  - Euler Product: ∏ (1 - p^-s)^-1 over all primes p
  - Analytic Continuation: Meromorphic function over C (pole at s=1)
  - Dirichlet Eta Function: Extends domain to Re(s) > 0
  - Functional Equation: Relates ζ(s) to ζ(1-s)
- Mathematical Origin & Purpose
  - Prime Number Distribution
  - Explicit Formula for π(x) (Prime-counting function)
  - Prime Power Function Π(x) & Möbius Inversion
  - Zeros Control Oscillations of Primes
  - Bridge: Discrete Number Theory & Continuous Complex Analysis
- Consequences & Equivalences
  - Distribution of Primes
    - Error Bound: |π(x) - li(x)| < (1/8π) √x log(x) (Schoenfeld/von Koch)
    - Cramér's Prime Gap Bounds
  - Growth of Arithmetic Functions
    - Mertens Function: M(x) = O(x^(1/2+ε))
    - Robin's Theorem: σ(n) < e^γ n log log n (n > 5040)
    - Lagarias Equivalent: Bound via Harmonic Numbers
  - Other Conjectures & Criteria
    - Lindelöf Hypothesis (Growth on Critical Line)
    - Riesz & Nyman-Beurling Criteria
    - de Bruijn–Newman Constant (Λ = 0)
- Location of Zeros & Verification
  - Density & Number of Zeros
    - N(T) ~ (T/2π) log(T/2π)
    - Hadamard & de la Vallée-Poussin: No zeros on Re(s) = 1
  - Zeros on Critical Line
    - Hardy (1914): Infinitely many zeros on Re(s) = 1/2
    - Selberg, Levinson, Conrey: Incremental proportion bounds
    - Anthropic Claude (2026): Proved > 66.6% (and ~67.25%) on critical line
    - Lamzouri (2026): Simplified Hilbert space proof for >2/3
  - Zero-Free Regions
    - Platt & Trudgian (2021): Verified up to t = 3 * 10^12
    - Mossinghoff, Trudgian, Yang (2022): Improved bounds
    - OpenAI (2026): Claimed proof for Re(s) > 7/8 (Quasi-RH)
  - Numerical Calculations & Phenomena
    - Gram Points & Gram's Law
    - Rosser's Rule
    - Lehmer's Phenomenon (Very close zero pairs)
- Generalizations & Analogues
  - Generalized Riemann Hypothesis (GRH) for Dirichlet L-functions
  - Extended RH (Dedekind Zeta Functions)
  - Grand RH (Automorphic L-functions / Selberg Class)
  - Weil Conjectures (Curves/Varieties over Finite Fields - Proved by Weil/Deligne)
